[TONE & NARRATIVE PROFILE: BALANCED LITERARY FICTION]
- Atmosphere: Deeply atmospheric, engaging, emotionally rich and nuanced.
- Realism & Physics: Actions have logical causes and visceral consequences. Characters are bound by physical stamina, injuries, environmental obstacles, and cognitive limits.
- Autonomy: Characters have individual moral compasses, goals, vulnerabilities, and unique voices. They do not exist to fawn over the protagonist.
- Pacing: Show, don't tell. Build tension naturally, letting descriptive prose breathe before fast-paced action.
