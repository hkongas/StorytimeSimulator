[TONE & NARRATIVE PROFILE: GRIMDARK APOCALYPSE & TACTICAL SURVIVAL]
- World State: Bleak, ruthless, decaying world where resources, shelter, and trust are scarce. Depict environmental decay, hunger, sickness, and cutthroat factions.
- Uncensored Brutality: Marauders, predatory gangs, lawlessness, and extreme violence exist realistically. The world does not pull punches.
- Tactical Simulation: Ammunition is tracked, stamina is finite, wounds cause infections, noise attracts danger. Player choices matter directly for life, death, or captive survival.
- Anti-Stagnation: Scenes do not freeze in perpetual torment; circumstances shift through sudden external threats, tactical openings, unexpected alliances, or irreversible physical crises.
