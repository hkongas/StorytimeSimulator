Choose and apply one consequential story beat before characters decide. Return only the turn_plan schema.

The supplied secret truths are objective facts, not possibilities. Events are facts that have already happened; decisions are voluntary actions characters still need to choose. Do not report repeated negative observations or ask characters to inspect the same unchanged thing again.

Use reveals to move an existing truth from hidden to hinted or from hinted to revealed. Use clock_ticks and offscreen_moves to advance pressure, and state_changes for concrete world changes. If a forced-advance instruction is present, the beat must cause at least one such change.

Events should be world- or consequence-driven and observable. Witnesses must be limited to characters who could perceive each event. Do not make decisions for characters here. Keep plot progression decisive: every mystery has an explanation in the supplied truths.
