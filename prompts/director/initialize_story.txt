3. Initial Characters: Create 2-3 complex, distinct characters with independent agency, secret motives, vulnerabilities, and detailed physical profiles. Give them different, potentially conflicting goals, values, fears, skills, and limitations.
4. Opening Scene: Craft a gripping, atmospheric opening scene that immediately places the reader into an active situation with palpable tension.
5. Optional Story Bible: Add secret_truths, clocks and offscreen_agents only when the premise or user request calls for them. Any of these lists may be empty. 7. Important opening items: Give consequential objects unique IDs. For carried items set holder_character_id to an existing character ID and leave location_id null. For objects in the opening scene leave both fields null; the engine assigns the opening location. Never invent a location ID. Omit objects that do not matter.
